POS Cash Out Restriction

This module allows you to restrict cash out operations in Odoo POS based on the actual cash available in the session. It prevents users from withdrawing more than what's currently in the cash register, improving control and preventing errors or fraud.

Key Features
  • Restrict cash out if the amount exceeds available cash.
  • Configurable restriction via POS settings.
  • Shows current available cash in the cash in/out popup.
  • Prevents operational mistakes or misuse.
How It Works

Once the module is installed, go to your POS settings and enable the "Cash Out Restriction" option. During a POS session, the system will prevent users from performing a cash out if the amount entered exceeds the available cash in the register. It also displays the available cash when performing a cash in/out operation.

POS Configuration

Enable the "Cash Out Restriction" option in your POS configuration to activate this feature.

POS View - Cash Restriction in Action

When performing a cash out, the system will display the available cash and restrict invalid amounts. It ensures secure cash management directly from the POS.
