POS Total Product Display

Functionality Overview

The POS Total Product Display feature provides a real-time count of all products available in the POS product screen. This helps cashiers, managers, and store owners quickly verify product availability without navigating through multiple screens.

Key Features

  • Displays the total number of products in the POS product screen.
  • Helps with inventory tracking and stock monitoring.
  • Ensures real-time updates when new products are added.
