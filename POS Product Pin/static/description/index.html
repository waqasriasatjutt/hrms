💡 How to Use Product Pin in POS

  1. Open Point of Sale → Products in your Odoo 18 POS backend.
  2. Click the pin icon on any product card or product form to pin or unpin it.
  3. Pinned products are visually highlighted with a pin icon in the POS product grid and always appear at the top for faster selection.
  4. Use the Pinned filter in the POS product screen to instantly view only your pinned products.
  5. Each POS user manages their own set of pinned products for a personalized and efficient POS experience.
  6. Pinned status is instantly updated across all POS sessions for the user.
💡 POS Tip:

Pin your most popular or frequently sold products to speed up checkout and reduce search time during busy hours.

⚙️ Installation Guide

Quick Installation Steps:

  1. Download the module to your Odoo 18 addons directory
  2. Update the Apps list in Odoo (Apps → Update Apps List)
  3. Search for "Product Pin" in the Apps menu
  4. Click Install and you're ready to go!
📋 Requirements:
  • Odoo Version: 18.0 or higher
  • Dependencies: None (uses core Odoo modules only)
  • Python: 3.10+ (standard with Odoo 18)
