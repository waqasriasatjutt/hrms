Car Consignment Management is designed for car dealerships and showrooms that sell vehicles on behalf of third-party owners. The module ensures that only your actual profit or commission is recorded as income, while keeping full visibility on vehicle cost, expenses, and selling price.

Key Features

  • Register cars as consignment products
  • Assign car owner and expected selling price
  • Track vehicle-related expenses (repairs, logistics, registration, etc.)
  • Generate customer invoices using a dedicated wizard
  • Automatically recognize profit only as company income
  • Seamless integration with Sales, Accounting, Stock & Website
  • Clear financial visibility per vehicle

Why Use This Module?

Traditional sales workflows do not support consignment-based business models well. This module fills that gap by providing a clean and compliant accounting flow where:

  • The car owner’s value is not treated as company revenue
  • Only the dealership’s margin is posted to income accounts
  • Expenses are transparently linked to each vehicle

Best Suited For

  • Car dealerships
  • Vehicle showrooms
  • Auto traders working on commission
  • Used car consignment businesses
