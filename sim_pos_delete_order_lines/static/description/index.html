Remove Order Line In POS

Tired of the slow and clunky backspace method to remove a product in your Odoo Point of Sale system? Our Remove Order Line in POS & Manage Orders app revolutionizes your checkout process by introducing a faster, more intuitive way to manage order lines. This module enhances the user interface by adding a simple "cross" icon to each order line, allowing cashiers to remove an item with a single click. Additionally, a "Clear All" button enables the instant cancellation of an entire order, dramatically speeding up operations and reducing errors. This is the essential order system upgrade for any modern retail POS system, looking to boost efficiency.

  • Add products to an order.
  • To remove one item, click the specific order line.
  • Press the 'Backspace' key on your keyboard. OR Press 'x' icon.
  • Repeat for every item you need to remove.

Add products to an order.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can I control which POS users are allowed to delete order lines?

Yes. The module respects Odoo’s access rights structure. Administrators can enable or restrict the ability to delete POS order lines by configuring user permissions, ensuring controlled and compliant operations.

Does deleting an order line update totals and taxes in real time?

Absolutely. When an order line is removed, the POS interface recalculates the subtotal, taxes, and final amount instantly, ensuring billing accuracy without requiring additional actions from the cashier.

Will this module affect previously validated POS orders or accounting entries?

No. The module only impacts order lines in the active POS session before order validation. Once a POS order is confirmed, its lines and related accounting entries remain unchanged to preserve financial integrity.
