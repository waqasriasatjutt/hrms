Detailed Features List

Hide Odoo Logo from POS Screen

  • This module removes visible Odoo logo from the PoS Screen.This gives your POS interface a cleaner, more professional appearance that aligns with your company's branding.

Remove "Powered by Odoo" Message on Receipts

  • By default, Odoo prints Powered by Odoo at the bottom of every POS receipt. This module automatically hides that line so your customers only see your brand on printed receipts.
