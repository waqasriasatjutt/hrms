Product Stock Alert (Low Stock)
Visual Low Stock Alerts in Products & POS
Stay Notified and Manage Inventory Efficiently

Enhance your Odoo inventory management with Product Stock Alert. This module provides visual indicators for low stock levels on product forms, tree/kanban views, and POS screens. Never run out of stock unnoticed and streamline your sales and warehouse operations.

Key features include:

  • ✅ Low Stock Indicators - Highlight products visually when stock is below threshold.
  • ✅ POS Alerts - Show alert badges directly in POS product cards.
  • ✅ Customizable Thresholds - Set minimum stock levels per product or globally.

  • ✓Compatible with POS

Install Product Stock Alert from Odoo Apps

Go to the Apps menu in Odoo and search for Product Stock Alert. Click on the module and install it to enable visual low stock alerts in Products and POS.

Enable Low Stock Alert from Settings

After installing the module, go to Settings > search for Products. You will see a checkbox labeled Low Stock Alert. Enable it, and an input field will appear where you can set the threshold quantity (e.g., 10). Products with stock below this number will show alerts in POS, Kanban, and List views.

See Low Stock Alerts in POS

After enabling the Low Stock Alert and setting the threshold, you can see visual warnings directly on products in the POS. The alert badge shows the current product quantity when it falls below the set threshold.

See Low Stock Alerts in Kanban View

After setting the Low Stock Alert, you can also view alerts on products in the Kanban view. Visual warnings make it easy to identify products that are below the stock threshold at a glance.

See Low Stock Alerts in List View

After enabling the Low Stock Alert, you can view warnings directly in the List view of products. This makes it easy to identify products below the threshold quantity at a glance.
