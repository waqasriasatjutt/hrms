WT Sales Analytics Dashboard

Enterprise Sales Analytics for Odoo 18 — 9 KPI cards, revenue trends, customer insights, product performance & period comparison.

Live Dashboard Preview

This is the actual dashboard — exactly what you see after installation.

Getting Started

Up and running in minutes.

1
Install Module

Install WT Sales Analytics Dashboard from Apps. Only Sales and Web are required — no extra dependencies.

2
Open Dashboard

From the Odoo home screen click Sales Analytics. The dashboard loads instantly with live data from all your sales orders.

3
Apply Filters

Use the top-bar filter strip to select a period, then optionally narrow by Salesperson and Customer. Click Refresh to reload all data.

4
Explore Tabs

Switch between tabs: Overview (revenue trend, order volume), Financial (margin, discount analysis), Customers (top buyers, new vs returning), Products (top sellers by qty & revenue) and Comparison (period vs period).

Module Features

Everything you need to understand your sales performance — all in one place.

📈
9 Live KPI Cards

Total Revenue, Orders, Avg Order Value, Items Sold, New Customers, Quotations, Confirmed Orders, Total Discounts and Tax Collected — all with period-over-period change indicators.

📊
Revenue Trend Charts

Daily, weekly or monthly revenue trend line overlaid with order volume bars. Spot seasonal patterns and growth trajectories at a glance.

👥
Customer Intelligence

Top customers by revenue, new vs returning breakdown, and customer lifetime value trends. Know exactly who drives your revenue.

🔍
Smart Filters

Filter by Today, Last 7 Days, This Month, This Quarter, This Year or a custom date range. Stack with Salesperson and Customer filters for precise revenue drilldowns.

🔁
Period Comparison

Compare current period revenue, order count, avg order value and items sold against the equivalent previous period with side-by-side bar charts and delta indicators.

📦
Product Performance

Top-selling products ranked by quantity and revenue. Identify your bestsellers, slow movers and high-margin lines in seconds.

📋
5 Analytical Tabs

Overview, Financial, Customers, Products and Comparison — each tab focuses on a specific dimension of your sales data with lazy loading for maximum performance.

💲
Financial Analytics

Gross margin by product category, discount impact analysis, and average revenue per customer — a full financial lens on your sales without leaving the dashboard.

📱
Fully Responsive

Works on desktop, tablet and mobile. KPI cards reflow from 3 columns to 2 to 1, tabs collapse and all charts resize fluidly.

How to Use — Step by Step

A complete guide to getting the most out of your sales analytics.

1
Install the Module

Go to Apps, search for WT Sales Analytics Dashboard and click Install. Only Sales and Web are required — all dependencies install automatically.

2
Open the Dashboard

From the Odoo home screen click the Sales Analytics app tile. The dashboard opens instantly, loading all KPIs, charts and data for the default period (Today). Access is restricted to users with the Sales / Manager or Salesperson role.

3
Set Your Filters

Use the top-bar filter strip to select a period, then optionally narrow by Salesperson and Customer. Click Refresh to reload all data.

4
Explore the Tabs

Switch between tabs: Overview (revenue trend, order volume), Financial (margin, discount analysis), Customers (top buyers, new vs returning), Products (top sellers by qty & revenue) and Comparison (period vs period).
