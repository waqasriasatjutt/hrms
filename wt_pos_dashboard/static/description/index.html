Module Features

Everything you need to understand your POS performance — all in one place.

📈
9 Live KPI Cards

Revenue, Orders, Average Order Value, Items Sold, Refunds, Open Sessions, Gross Margin, Total Discounts and Tax Collected — all shown with period-over-period change indicators (▲ / ▼).

📊
7 Interactive Charts

Sales Trend, Payment Breakdown, Revenue vs Refunds, Hourly Heatmap, Top Products (qty & revenue), Top Categories, Top Customers, New vs Returning and Period Comparison — all powered by Chart.js 4.

📋
6 Analytical Tabs

Overview, Financial, Products, Customers, Sessions and Comparison — each tab focuses on a specific dimension of your POS data with lazy loading for maximum performance.

🔍
Smart Filters

Filter by Today, Last 7 Days, This Month, This Quarter, This Year or a custom date range. Stack with POS terminal and cashier filters for precise drilldowns. All charts refresh instantly.

🔁
Period Comparison

Compare current period revenue, orders, average order and items sold against the equivalent previous period. Summary cards and a side-by-side bar chart show exactly where performance improved or declined.

📄
CSV Export

Download every filtered order line as a CSV with a single click. BOM-prefixed for perfect Excel compatibility. Includes order ref, date, product, qty, price, discount, tax, cashier and payment method.

👥
Customer Intelligence

Track top customers by revenue, segment buyers into New, Returning and Walk-in, and visualise trends over time. Identify your most loyal customers at a glance.

💲
Financial Analytics

Discounts by Cashier, Gross Margin by Category, Average Revenue by Hour and Top Discounted Products — a full financial lens on your POS operations without leaving the dashboard.

📱
Fully Responsive

Works on desktop, tablet and mobile. KPI cards reflow from 3 columns to 2 to 1, tabs collapse and all charts resize fluidly. Manage your POS insights from any device.

How to Use — Step by Step

A complete guide to getting the most out of your POS dashboard.

1
Install the Module

Go to Apps, search for WT POS Analytics Dashboard and click Install. Only Point of Sale and Web are required — all dependencies install automatically.

2
Open the Dashboard

From the Odoo home screen click the POS Analytics app tile. The dashboard opens instantly, loading all KPIs, charts and the orders table for the default period (Today). Access is restricted to users with the Point of Sale / Manager role.

3
Set Your Filters

Use the top-bar filter strip to select a period (Today, Last 7 Days, This Month, This Quarter, This Year or Custom Range), then optionally narrow by POS Terminal and Cashier. Click Refresh to reload all data with your selected filters applied.

4
Explore the Tabs

Switch between the six tabs to drill into your data: Overview (sales trend, payments, heatmap, recent orders), Financial (discounts by cashier, margin by category, avg revenue by hour), Products (top products by qty & revenue, top categories), Customers (top customers, new vs returning), Sessions (open/closed session status) and Comparison (current vs previous period side-by-side).

5
Export Your Data

Click the green Export CSV button to download all filtered order lines as a spreadsheet. The file is BOM-prefixed for perfect Excel compatibility and includes: order reference, date, product, category, quantity, unit price, discount, tax, subtotal, cashier and payment method.
